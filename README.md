# Konnekt

**A music player for Android, featuring a polished interface and flexible music sources.**


</div>

Konnekt brings together music search and playback through YouTube Music, local audio files, and configurable music sources in a single application. It offers background playback, synchronized lyrics, and advanced audio settings.

## Features

- **Playback:** Music search, local library, background playback, offline downloads, crossfade, gapless playback, and sleep timer.
- **Lyrics & Interface:** Synchronized lyrics, animated player interface, album artwork-inspired colors, and detailed audio stream information.
- **Sources & Accounts:** Configurable additional music sources, Google sign-in for personalized content, Spotify playlist integration, and Last.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbling.
- **Social Features:** Discord Rich Presence and group listening with Listen Together.
- **Audio Settings:** Separate audio quality settings for Wi-Fi and mobile data, playback speed control, equalizer, and customizable playback options.

Some features require an account, a configured music source, or an external service.
